Typical flow

  1. Call domain_provider to find who manages the domain's DNS.
  2. Add the A, CNAME and TXT records at that DNS provider (or through the /v1/connect/direct API for AWS Route 53 and Hostinger).
  3. Call domain_status to confirm the A, CNAME and TXT records have propagated.
  4. Call domain_verify to mark the domain verified on your account.
